What is 2+2?

Two colleges, one degree.

A 2+2 pathway means two years at a community college, then two years at a four‑year university, finishing the same bachelor's degree. Community colleges are officially recognized, two‑year U.S. schools — smaller classes, lower cost, and a good place to build your skills and confidence before you transfer. When credits transfer under a real credit‑transfer agreement, the diploma reads as a straight degree from the university. Where a student started isn't on it.

The 2+2 Pathway

What is 2+2, and how is it different from starting at a university?

A full explanation of the 2+2 model, how it compares to enrolling directly at a four‑year university, and exactly what's included when you choose this route with us.

The simple version

"2+2" means two years at a community college, then two years at a four‑year university — finishing with the same bachelor's degree a student who started at the university would get.

A community college is an officially recognized, two‑year U.S. school. Classes are smaller, tuition is lower, and it's a good place to build academic skills and confidence before the pace picks up at a university.

The diploma comes from the university. When credits transfer under a real credit‑transfer agreement, the degree reads as a normal degree from that university. Where the student started isn't printed on it, and no one asks.

What we actually do for a 2+2 student

If you choose the 2+2 route inside our Full‑Service Pathway package, here's exactly what's included:

  • We manage your application to a partner community college and get you admitted.
  • 6 advising sessions (45 min), covering college matching, document prep, and transfer planning.
  • We build your plan around a real, named transfer system — not a promise we made up: California (community college → UC/CSU), Washington (Direct Transfer Agreement), Texas (statewide core), or Massachusetts (MassTransfer).
  • Visa interview prep and ongoing communication with your college.
  • 90 days of on‑campus support after you enroll.

Year‑two transfer support — helping you prepare and submit the actual transfer application when the time comes — is a separate, paid add‑on. It isn't included automatically in the $2,000 package, since a student's plans and timeline can change over two years.

Is the transfer guaranteed?

No — and we won't tell you it is. Transfer credit is decided by the receiving university's registrar, based on its own published transfer agreement. That's exactly why we only build pathways around real, named state transfer systems, instead of a vague promise that "credits usually transfer."

What we guarantee is narrower and real: if you complete the Full‑Service Pathway package and aren't admitted to any community college you applied to through us, we refund the fee in full. See our money‑back guarantee for details.

Why a foundation is doing this.

Eurasia Foundation is a non‑profit international development organization dedicated to engaging citizens and empowering communities globally. For over three decades, we've worked directly in the communities we serve — not as a distant funder, but as a partner on the ground. That commitment expanded into Central Asia in 2019, reaching Kazakhstan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, and Uzbekistan with locally led development work. Academic Horizons USA grew out of a simple observation from that work: talented students across Central Asia have real paths to U.S. colleges, and smaller U.S. colleges have real room for them, but almost nobody is connecting the two honestly. So we built the connection ourselves, using the same standards of transparency and accountability that have defined our work since 1992.

Terms of Service

Last updated: September 2026

These terms cover your use of Academic Horizons USA's advising services and this website. By booking a consultation or purchasing a package, you agree to them.

Who can use our services

Our services, including the free consultation, are for students who are 18 years of age or older. We are not able to advise or accept payment on behalf of a student under 18.

Our services

We provide college matching, application preparation, document review, and advising sessions, at the level described in the package you purchase. We prepare your application as strongly as we can — but admission and visa decisions are made by colleges, universities, and the U.S. government, not by us.

Pricing and payment

Package fees cover our advising services only. Tuition, application fees paid directly to a college or university, visa fees, housing deposits, and living costs are separate, are paid directly to those institutions or agencies, and are not included in or refundable through us.

Money‑back guarantee

If you purchase a Full‑Service Pathway or Direct Admission Premium package, we guarantee a full refund of your program fee if you are not admitted to any of the institutions we submitted an application to on your behalf.

This guarantee covers admission only. It does not cover a visa being refused after admission — the visa interview is a separate decision made entirely by the U.S. Consulate, after the college has already said yes.

To request a refund, contact your advisor within 30 days of receiving the final admission decision. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days of approval.

What this guarantee does not cover

  • A visa being refused after admission — see above. If a student is admitted and the visa is later refused, the package is considered delivered.
  • Pathway Starter is advice‑only — no applications are submitted on your behalf — so it is not eligible for this guarantee.
  • Complimentary Consultation is free and carries no fee to refund.
  • Costs paid to other organizations (application fees, tuition, visa fees, housing deposits) are not covered, since we do not control or collect them.
  • The guarantee does not apply if a family withdraws an application, misses a required deadline, or provides incomplete or inaccurate information after being advised otherwise.

No guarantee of outcomes beyond this policy

Outside of the money‑back guarantee above, we do not and cannot promise admission, a visa, transfer credit, scholarships, or any other outcome that is not ours to decide. If a stronger promise appears anywhere else on this site, it is a mistake — this page states our real policy.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as our services or partner agreements change. Important changes will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
