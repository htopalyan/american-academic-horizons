What is 2+2?

Two colleges, one degree.

A 2+2 pathway means two years at a community college, then two years at a four‑year university, finishing the same bachelor's degree. Community colleges are accredited, two‑year U.S. institutions — smaller classes, lower cost, and an easier place to build academic footing before transferring. When credits transfer under a real articulation agreement, the diploma reads as a straight degree from the university. Where a student started isn't on it.

Cost matters to your family

Two years of community college tuition run well below university tuition, without changing the degree at the end.

You need a grade runway

A stronger transcript from community college can open doors to a more selective university than direct admission would.

You're adjusting to the U.S. system

Smaller classes and closer advising make the first year or two an easier place to learn how American higher education works.

Your English is conversational, not yet academic

Community college gives room to build academic English before the pace picks up at a university.

We can't guarantee an admission decision or a visa outcome — those belong to the college and the U.S. government, not to us. Our money‑back guarantee covers what we can promise: if either doesn't go your way, you don't pay for the attempt. College placement fees are billed to partner institutions once a student enrolls, never added to your family's price.
Where You Can Study

Find the U.S. Education Experience That Fits You

Have you ever felt like you were meant to go further than your hometown?

Maybe you want a world‑recognized degree that opens doors internationally. Maybe you're looking for access to opportunities you can't find at home. Or maybe you've simply imagined yourself having the American college experience you've seen in movies.

Whatever brings you here, your path to studying in the United States starts with a much more important question than "Where should I study?" What do you want your future to look like?

There is more than one path

Where are you in your education today? How strong is your academic record? What have you done outside the classroom? What do you want to study, and what would you like to do afterward?

Your answers help determine the path that makes the most sense for you.

U.S. higher education offers many possibilities. You might begin at a community college and earn an associate degree, choose a specialized career‑focused program, or use community college as an affordable pathway to transfer to a four‑year institution. You might apply directly to a college or university for the full four‑year undergraduate experience. And later, graduate and professional programs can take your education even further.

There isn't one right route. There is a route that is right for you.

Now imagine where you would be happiest

Choosing a university isn't only about rankings and academics. You will be building a life there, too.

Would you thrive at a large university with 50,000 students, major sports, hundreds of student organizations, and something happening every day? Or would you feel more comfortable at a smaller college where professors know your name and the campus feels like a close community?

Can you picture yourself studying in the middle of a major city surrounded by people from all over the world? Or would you rather experience life in a smaller American college town?

Would you love to live near the ocean? Experience four distinct seasons? See snow for the first time? Or spend your college years somewhere warm and sunny all year round?

And what else matters to you — campus life, sports, research opportunities, internships, safety, cost, cultural diversity, proximity to an airport, or having an international community nearby?

Your priorities become part of the search

We look at the whole picture: your academics, interests, career goals, budget, preferred environment, and the kind of experience you want to have.

Then we help you explore institutions and pathways where those pieces come together.

Because finding the right fit means more than getting admitted. We want you to find a place where you can succeed academically, move toward the career you envision, build relationships, experience life in the United States, and genuinely enjoy the years you spend there.

Your U.S. education should fit your ambitions, your circumstances, and you.

Why a foundation is doing this.

Eurasia Foundation is a non‑profit international development organization dedicated to engaging citizens and empowering communities globally. For over three decades, we've worked directly in the communities we serve — not as a distant funder, but as a partner on the ground. That commitment expanded into Central Asia in 2019, reaching Kazakhstan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, and Uzbekistan with locally led development work. American Academic Horizons grew out of a simple observation from that work: talented students across Central Asia have real paths to U.S. colleges, and smaller U.S. colleges have real room for them, but almost nobody is connecting the two honestly. So we built the connection ourselves, using the same standards of transparency and accountability that have defined our work since 1992.

Our services

Pricing and payment

Package fees cover our advising services only. Tuition, application fees paid directly to a college or university, visa fees, housing deposits, and living costs are separate, are paid directly to those institutions or agencies, and are not included in or refundable through us.

Money‑back guarantee

If you purchase a Full‑Service Pathway or Direct Admission Premium package, we guarantee a full refund of your program fee if either of the following happens:

  • You are not admitted to any of the institutions we submitted an application to on your behalf, or
  • You attend your visa interview and are not issued a visa.

To request a refund, contact your advisor within 30 days of receiving the final admission decision or your visa interview outcome. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days of approval.

What this guarantee does not cover

  • Pathway Starter is advice‑only — no applications are submitted on your behalf — so it is not eligible for this guarantee.
  • Complimentary Consultation is free and carries no fee to refund.
  • Third‑party costs (application fees, tuition, visa fees, housing deposits) are not covered, since we do not control or collect them.
  • The guarantee does not apply if a family withdraws an application, misses a required deadline, or provides incomplete or inaccurate information after being advised otherwise.

No guarantee of outcomes beyond this policy

Outside of the money‑back guarantee above, we do not and cannot promise admission, a visa, transfer credit, scholarships, or any other outcome controlled by a third party. If a claim like that appears anywhere else on this site, this policy governs.
